About Trusteel

A supply platform, not a spot broker.

Established in 2024 as part of a large-scale steel manufacturing and distribution group, Trusteel currently supplies buyers in more than 10 countries across the Middle East and Africa.

01
Who We Are

One steel partner. Multiple mills.

Group manufacturing capacity plus multiple qualified partner mills — consolidated into one commercial relationship for importers, distributors and project buyers.

A

Group Manufacturing Backing

Part of a large-scale steel manufacturing and distribution group. Group production lines span straight-seam welded pipes, hot-dip galvanized pipes, square and rectangular tubes, spiral welded pipe, stainless steel pipes and fittings, ringlock scaffolding systems and oil pipes.

B

Regional Focus

Products supplied and exported to more than 10 countries across the Middle East and Africa — positioned for active regional distribution and construction demand, with commercial coordination through Dubai.

C

Commercial Approach

Professional communication, direct terms, and a long-term cooperation mindset with both mills and buyers. A multilingual international Business Development Team develops direct relationships with distributors and importers across the region.

D

Operational Reality

Loading supervision, container programs, mill certificates and third-party inspection — the unglamorous details that make international steel supply reliable.
