How Credit Works

Structured, approved, documented.

Credit at Trusteel is a commercial tool for established relationships — not a marketing promise.

01

Qualification & Verification

Buyers share company background, trading history and references. For qualified long-term customers, our Business Development Team conducts a physical verification visit at your premises — understanding your operations, market position, purchasing requirements and long-term business potential.

02

Approval

Following successful verification and approval, eligible customers may receive structured payment terms designed to support purchasing capacity and business growth — amount, tenor, documentation and security as appropriate to the transaction structure.

03

Confirmation

Every approved structure is confirmed in writing. Where a specific approved transaction carries a defined term — for example, a specific tenor on that transaction — that term is stated for that transaction.

Our credit commitment. Flexible credit terms may be available for qualified buyers, subject to credit approval and transaction structure. All terms are confirmed in writing per transaction. We do not advertise guaranteed tenors — we structure and confirm real terms for real transactions.
